Direction

Checkpoint 01 / November 2026

A lost 16-bit adventure cartridge made by a contemporary aerospace design studio: warm, tactile and hand-built, with interfaces that are crisp, technical and calm. The palette is limited, outlines are selective, and every room explains what people do there before a line of dialogue is read.

What this checkpoint proves

One coherent visual language across three authored environments, five fictional characters, two 64-tile atlases, a six-wheel rover, instrument art and a complete interface kit.

SELECTIVE OUTLINES / UPPER-LEFT KEY LIGHT / HARD ALPHA / NO BAKED SHADOWS ON CHARACTERS

Originality boundary

Every raster asset is drawn by checked-in Pillow scripts. No generative image model, third-party sprite pack, NASA photograph, logo, typeface, mission patch or employee likeness is used. Mars colour is a reference, not sampled imagery.

FICTIONAL CAST / FICTIONAL HARDWARE / SIMPLIFIED EDUCATIONAL GAMEPLAY VALUES

Approval gate

Production integration is deliberately paused. If this direction is approved, implementation continues from these assets instead of restarting planning or replacing the art direction.
